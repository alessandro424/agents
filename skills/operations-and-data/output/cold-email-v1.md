## Strategy Notes (Based on `cold-email` skill):

1.  **Subject Line:** Short, lowercase, and looks like an internal note about a process.
2.  **The "Why Now":** Mentioning SES.XIX (Real Decreto 933/2021) creates immediate relevance for Spanish hoteliers.
3.  **The Shift:** Focuses on the "manual burden" (carga manual) vs. "automation" (automatización).
4.  **Low Friction CTA:** Doesn't ask for a meeting yet, just asks if it "makes sense" (¿Tendría sentido...?) to see how it works.
5.  **No Fluff:** No "Espero que estés bien" or "Mi nombre es...". Straight to the point.
